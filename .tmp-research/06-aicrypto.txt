Here are the top AI x Crypto developments from the week of March 16–22, 2026, focusing on AI agent tokens, compute networks, and AI-integrated protocols:

### 1. Bittensor (TAO) Achieves Record Decentralized Training & Institutional Milestone
* **Dates:** March 14–21, 2026
* **Sector:** AI Protocols / Compute Networks
* **Details:** Bittensor's Subnet 3 (τemplar) successfully completed the pre-training of **Covenant-72B**, a 72-billion-parameter language model. This stands as the largest decentralized LLM training run on record, utilizing over 70 global contributors running commodity hardware. Furthermore, on March 21, Grayscale filed a Form S-1 with the SEC to convert its Bittensor Trust into a spot Exchange-Traded Product (ETP) on the NYSE Arca (ticker: GTAO), bringing major institutional legitimacy to the network. TAO surged over 56% during the week.
* **Sources:** CoinMarketCap [1], MEXC [2, 3]

### 2. Akash Network (AKT) Prepares for Deflationary "Burn-Mint Equilibrium" Hard Fork
* **Dates:** March 16–21, 2026 (Upgrade scheduled for March 23)
* **Sector:** Compute Networks
* **Details:** Akash Network dominated the decentralized physical infrastructure network (DePIN) narrative by finalizing preparations for its Burn-Mint Equilibrium (BME) upgrade. The hard fork introduces a permanent burn mechanism for AKT used in compute workloads, making the token supply deflationary and directly tied to network usage, while also adding full WASM smart contract support. The anticipation drove AKT prices up nearly 20% over the week.
* **Sources:** OurCryptoTalk [6], Identosphere [4], CoinMarketCap [7]
