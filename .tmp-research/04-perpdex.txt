Based on the latest reports and data from sources like Messari and The Block, here are the top developments in the perpetual DEX landscape for the week of March 23–29, 2026:

**1. Hyperliquid Solidifies Dominance with TradFi Integration**
Hyperliquid has emerged as the clear market leader, capturing between **44% and 54%** of the total perpetual DEX market share. The platform's success is heavily driven by non-crypto assets; its crude oil perpetuals saw daily volumes exceed $2.2 billion. Crucially, Hyperliquid launched the first official S&P 500 perpetual contract in partnership with S&P Dow Jones Indices, and Grayscale filed an S-1 for a spot HYPE ETF (ticker: GHYP), signaling massive institutional adoption. 

**2. GMX Executes a "Corporate Pivot" to Regain Relevance**
With its market share falling below 3%, GMX is undergoing a radical restructuring. The GMX DAO officially opened applications for a professional CEO to transition away from a pure DAO structure toward a corporate execution model. To stabilize its token, GMX implemented a 1 million token buy-wall at $5 and paused staking rewards, redirecting all protocol fees to treasury buybacks until a $90 token price target is met.

**3. dYdX Pivots to Heavy Value Accrual and RWAs**
Once the market leader, dYdX's dominance has slipped below 5%. In response to criticisms of its "high FDV, low float" tokenomics, the community approved an aggressive plan to scale token buybacks to **75% of net protocol revenue**. To compete with Hyperliquid's commodity success, dYdX also announced plans to launch synthetic equity perpetuals, starting with Tesla.

**4. The Rise of "Aster" and the Hybrid Dominance**
Data from The Block and DefiLlama indicates that the "Hybrid" model (on-chain settlement with off-chain performance) now accounts for 70–80% of all perpetual DEX volume. A new hybrid contender, **Aster**, has quietly surged to take the #2 spot in the market with roughly 15–20% market share, officially outpacing legacy giants like dYdX and GMX.
