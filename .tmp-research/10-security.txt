Here are the most important crypto security incidents, hacks, and exploits that occurred during the week of **March 23–29, 2026**:

### Major Hacks & Exploits

**1. Iranian Cryptocurrency Exchange Hack**
*   **Date Reported:** March 27, 2026
*   **Estimated Loss:** ~$90 Million
*   **Details:** Hackers drained approximately $90 million from the largest cryptocurrency exchange in Iran. Security analysts strongly suspect this massive breach was tied to escalating geopolitical tensions and state-sponsored activity in the region. 

**2. Resolv Labs (USR Stablecoin) Exploit**
*   **Date:** March 22–24, 2026
*   **Estimated Loss:** ~$25 Million
*   **Details:** Attackers compromised a privileged private key stored in Resolv's AWS Key Management Service (KMS). This allowed them to mint 80 million unbacked USR stablecoin tokens with only $200,000 in collateral. The attacker swapped the minted tokens for roughly 11,400 ETH, effectively stealing $25 million and causing the USR stablecoin to de-peg and crash to $0.025. By March 26, the team successfully blacklisted/burned 46 million USR to mitigate further damage.

**3. Infiniti Stealer & GhostClaw Malware Campaigns**
*   **Date:** March 28–29, 2026
*   **Estimated Loss:** Undisclosed (Ongoing Wallet Drains)
*   **Details:** Cybersecurity researchers identified two new severe threats targeting crypto users and developers. "Infiniti Stealer" targeted macOS users via fake Cloudflare CAPTCHA pages to instantly drain browser wallets. Concurrently, "GhostClaw" malware was found hidden within malicious `npm` packages, designed to hunt and steal developers' private keys.

**4. Fake Stablecoin Proliferation**
*   **Date Reported:** March 27, 2026
*   **Details:** Security firm Blockaid reported a massive spike in phishing infrastructure, revealing that over 54,000 fake stablecoins (mostly mimicking USDT and USDC) had been deployed to trick users into signing malicious approvals and draining their wallets.

### Legacy Hack Developments & Fallout

While the initial breaches didn't happen this week, significant corporate and financial fallout related to massive historical hacks occurred:

*   **Balancer Labs Shutdown ($128 Million Fallout):** On March 23–24, Balancer Labs announced it was winding down its corporate entity entirely. The shutdown was attributed to the "unbearable legal liability" and loss of revenue stemming from their massive $128 million protocol exploit in November 2025. (The protocol remains functional under a DAO).
*   **Bo Shen Bounty ($42 Million Recovery Effort):** On March 26, Fenbushi Capital founder Bo Shen publicly offered a 10–20% bounty for information leading to the recovery of $42 million stolen from his personal wallet in 2022, following new breakthroughs in tracking the stolen funds.
