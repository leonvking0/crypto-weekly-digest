### 2. ECB Targets "De-Pseudo-Centralization" in Blue Chips (March 27, 2026)
*   **Category:** Governance / Regulation
*   **Development:** The European Central Bank (ECB) released a landmark report placing the DeFi sector under a strict regulatory microscope. The report specifically named blue-chip protocols **Aave, MakerDAO, and Uniswap**, arguing they are not truly decentralized. The ECB highlighted that the top 100 governance token holders across these protocols routinely control over 80% of voting power, categorizing them as "centralized intermediaries" that should be subjected to MiCA-style accountability and oversight.
*   **Implied Sources:** CoinDesk, Messari Regulatory Research.

### 3. Resolv (USR) Exploit & Morpho Yield Contagion (March 24-28, 2026)
*   **Category:** Yield Developments / Security
*   **Development:** Delta-neutral stablecoin protocol Resolv was exploited for $23.6 million due to a compromised private key. The attacker minted $80 million in unbacked USR and dumped it across DEXs, crashing the token's price to $0.05. This triggered a severe yield contagion event on **Morpho**, where several lending vaults were completely drained because their oracles continued to value the collapsed USR at a hardcoded $1.13 price. This exploit pushed total DeFi losses for Q1 2026 past the $137 million mark.
*   **Implied Sources:** The Block, DeFi Security Reports.

### 4. TVL Stabilizes at $93B Amidst Shifting DEX Dominance (Late March 2026)
*   **Category:** TVL Movements / Market Pulse
*   **Development:** Total Value Locked (TVL) in DeFi stabilized around **$93 billion**, representing a healthy consolidation backed by higher-quality collateral following the $180 billion peak in late 2025. A notable shift in market structure occurred as decentralized perpetual exchange **Hyperliquid** logged $6.4 billion in daily volume, massively outperforming **Uniswap’s** $1 billion in spot volume. In terms of chain dominance, Ethereum maintained ~60% ($57.4B), while Solana ($7.0B) and Base ($5.3B) cemented themselves as the primary high-throughput hubs.
*   **Implied Sources:** Messari Data, The Block Data Dashboard.

### 5. I-ON Digital & pmUSD Expand RWA Yield (March 26, 2026)
*   **Category:** Protocol Launches / Real-World Assets (RWA)
*   **Development:** Following the momentum of the mid-March launch of BlackRock's Staked ETH ETF and Nasdaq's SEC approval for trading tokenized securities, a major new partnership was unveiled between I-ON Digital and the **pmUSD** collateralized stablecoin. The launch bridges tokenized gold-backed assets with DeFi liquidity. This underscores the broader March 2026 yield trend: capital is aggressively rotating out of inflationary token rewards (the old "liquidity wars") and into sustainable, fixed-rate, and RWA-backed vault standards (like ERC-4626/ERC-7540).
*   **Implied Sources:** CoinDesk, Messari Institutional Reports.
