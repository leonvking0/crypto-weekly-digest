These bills are designed to explicitly prohibit government officials from using non-public information to place trades on platforms like Polymarket.

In anticipation of the aforementioned federal oversight, both Polymarket and Kalshi proactively updated their internal terms of service on March 23. The new rules explicitly ban traders from utilizing confidential or non-public information to participate in prediction markets.
* **Source:** CryptoNews [8]

### 3. Record-Breaking Volume Reaches $9.7 Billion (March 2026)
Polymarket's sustained growth culminated in a massive **$9.7 billion 30-day trading volume** by mid-to-late March. * **Source:** MetaMask / QCX [7]
