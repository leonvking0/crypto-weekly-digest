Here are the most important Layer 1 and Layer 2 developments from the past 7 days (early April 2026), synthesized primarily from **The Block** and **Messari** reporting:

### 1. Circle Unveils Quantum-Resistant L1 "Arc"
* **Date:** Early April 2026
* **Description:** Circle revealed the roadmap for its upcoming proprietary Layer 1 blockchain, Arc. The network is built to be quantum-resistant from launch, integrating a post-quantum signature scheme designed to protect the chain against future advanced decryption threats.
* **Source:** [The Block](https://theblock.co)

### 2. Base Establishes Dominance as L2 Market Consolidates ($5.6B TVL)
* **Date:** April 2026
* **Description:** The Layer 2 landscape has heavily consolidated into a "three-horse race" between Base, Arbitrum, and Optimism. Base hit a massive milestone this week, capturing a **$5.6 billion DeFi TVL** (46.6% market share) and processing roughly 60% of all L2 transactions. Meanwhile, Arbitrum maintained its dominance in institutional Total Value Secured (TVS) at over $20 billion.
* **Source:** [The Block](https://theblock.co)

### 3. SKALE Launches on Base (L3) to Support AI Agent Workloads
* **Date:** Early April 2026
* **Description:** Following Messari's thesis that AI agent workloads will drive future on-chain demand, "SKALE on Base" officially launched. Purpose-built as an Ethereum Layer 3 for AI, it utilizes the x402 payments protocol to allow AI agents to operate on-chain autonomously without managing gas fees.
* **Source:** [The Block](https://theblock.co)

### 4. Ether.fi Migrates $5.7B Ecosystem to Optimism (OP Mainnet)
* **Date:** Early April 2026
* **Description:** In one of the most significant recent TVL shifts, prominent restaking protocol Ether.fi migrated its $5.7 billion ecosystem—including its associated credit card product—from Scroll to OP Mainnet. The move highlights an ongoing trend of high-profile protocols consolidating onto top-tier L2s for deeper native liquidity. 
* **Source:** [The Block](https://theblock.co)

### 5. Cardano Prepares for Protocol 11 Hard Fork Amid Record Dev Activity
* **Date:** April 2026
* **Description:** In preparation for its major Protocol 11 hard fork (which implements substantial network governance upgrades), Messari data tracked a massive surge in network building. Cardano led all major blockchains in developer activity this past week, recording over 680 weekly commits.
