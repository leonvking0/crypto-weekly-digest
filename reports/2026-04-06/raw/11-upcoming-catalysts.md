### 1. CLARITY Act Senate Markup (Regulatory Catalyst)
*   **Date:** Expected April 13–18, 2026
*   **Affected Assets:** Broad Market, Stablecoins, DeFi blue-chips (e.g., $UNI, $AAVE, $MKR)
*   **Why it matters:** The U.S. Senate Banking Committee is expected to mark up the CLARITY Act, a critical piece of legislation that specifically addresses stablecoin yields and the legal classification of DeFi protocols. Analysts highlight this as one of the most defining U.S. regulatory events of the year, as it will likely establish the structural and legal baseline for digital assets for the rest of the decade.
