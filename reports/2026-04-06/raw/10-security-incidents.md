Here are the top crypto security incidents and related developments from the past 7 days (April 5–12, 2026), based on reports from The Block, Messari analysts, and other major outlets:

### 1. Drift Protocol Post-Mortem Reveals $285M Social Engineering Exploit
*   **Date of Report:** April 5, 2026 (Incident occurred April 1)
*   **Loss Amount:** $280M – $285M
*   **Description:** Following the largest DeFi hack of 2026, Drift Protocol published a detailed post-mortem. The attackers, suspected to be North Korean-aligned, executed a six-month "structured intelligence operation." They posed as a quantitative trading firm, deposited $1M of their own capital to build trust, and attended international conferences with Drift contributors. Using a novel exploit involving Solana "durable nonces," they tricked Security Council members into pre-signing transactions, eventually whitelisting a fake token to drain over 50% of the protocol's TVL in 12 minutes. Messari analysts noted this event "resets trust for six months" within the Solana ecosystem.
*   **Sources:** [The Block](https://www.theblock.co), [Chainalysis](https://www.chainalysis.com)

### 2. Resolv Labs AWS Infrastructure Breach and Minting Exploit
*   **Date Reported:** Early April 2026
*   **Loss Amount:** ~$25M
*   **Description:** Stablecoin issuer Resolv Labs suffered a severe exploit after attackers compromised the project's AWS Key Management Service (KMS) infrastructure. The breach allowed the attackers to exploit a flaw in the protocol's USR minting contract, generating 80 million unbacked stablecoins and resulting in approximately $25 million in losses. 
*   **Sources:** [The Block](https://www.theblock.co)
